Switching browsers shouldn’t mean rebuilding your world from memory.

  1. Choose your route.

    Connect a specific source and destination profile with the local connector. Choose a private backup folder once, then preview what the route supports.

  2. Make room for a safe move.

    Click Transfer on an eligible route. Browser Bridge captures, requests a normal quit, makes checksummed backups and reopens the destination. It pauses for prompts and never force-quits.

  3. Bring it across.

    Review the transfer receipt and verification result. Keep the original backup. Other routes continue to use target-specific helpers or guided file imports.

Connect once. Then Transfer.

The beta’s verified automatic route is Chrome 156.0.8078.48 → Brave 156.1.98.60 on Apple silicon. Intel uses guided transfer; Intel runtime is unverified. Other versions, directions and browser pairs remain guided. Local connector setup and browser permissions are required.

Browser Current route Capture & import boundary

Verified: Chrome → Brave

Chrome 156.0.8078.48 → Brave 156.1.98.60. A disposable-profile end-to-end run verified 11 imported bookmarks, 2 tabs, 1 group and 3 history entries, with zero verification mismatches. The destination’s existing 12 bookmarks and 3 tabs remained. This evidence covers that direction and those versions on Apple silicon only. The universal app includes Intel, but Intel runtime is unverified and its transfers remain guided.

The details matter.

Bookmarks and folders are portable. Tabs, pins, groups and history depend on exposed browser APIs and the selected route. Native Spaces, Essentials and Favorites may become folders or stay in the archive; exact parity is not promised.

Passwords, passkeys, active cookie sessions and incompatible extension data need the browser’s own tools. Nothing on this website can access them.
